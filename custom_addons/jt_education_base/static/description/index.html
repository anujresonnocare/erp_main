1. Overview

Education Management ERP is a comprehensive Odoo solution designed to simplify and automate the day-to-day operations of schools, colleges, universities, coaching institutes, and other educational organizations. It centralizes academic, administrative, and financial processes into one powerful platform, allowing institutions to efficiently manage their complete educational ecosystem.

The application covers every stage of the student lifecycle, from admission inquiries and enrollment to attendance tracking, examinations, assignments, fee collection, library management, transportation, hostel management, counseling, events, and academic reporting. Faculty, parents, administrators, and students can all collaborate through a unified system, reducing manual work while improving communication and transparency.

Built on Odoo, the system offers a modern and user-friendly interface with integrated reporting, configurable workflows, and role-based access. Whether managing a small institute or a multi-campus educational organization, Education Management ERP provides the flexibility and scalability required to streamline operations and enhance the overall learning experience.


2. Features

  • Streamline the complete admission process, from inquiry registration to student enrollment with organized records.
  • Maintain comprehensive student profiles with academic, personal, health, fee, and parent information.
  • Manage faculty records, qualifications, health details, work experience, and academic responsibilities from one place.
  • Record attendance, create class timetables, and efficiently organize daily academic schedules.
  • Schedule examinations, generate student results automatically, calculate grades, and print professional report cards.
  • Create assignments, monitor submissions, evaluate student performance, and track completion status with ease.
  • Handle student fees, quotations, payment tracking, and outstanding balances with integrated financial management.
  • Organize library books, memberships, issue and return records, and detailed library reports.
  • Manage hostel registrations, room allocation, housekeeping, student complaints, and parent meetings.
  • Configure transportation routes, vehicles, drivers, conductors, fare structures, and student transport assignments.
  • Conduct counseling sessions, collect feedback, manage surveys, and improve student well-being through structured records.
  • Organize educational events, manage registrations, ticketing, attendee tracking, and event communication.
  • Generate attendance, examination, evaluation, fee, and academic reports to support informed decision-making.
  • Includes a dedicated mobile application for students, parents, and faculty to stay connected anytime, anywhere.
